        summary:
          - "Amazon VP and Distinguished Engineer Terk Terkowitz published updated Software Development Engineer role guidelines — the first formal SDE role redefinition from a major tech company that explicitly accounts for AI adoption. The core position: measure engineers by outcomes (customer and business impact), not by token usage, lines of code, or AI tool adoption rate. The guidelines explicitly warn against 'tokenmaxxing' — optimising for AI interaction volume as a proxy for productivity — calling it 'deeply flawed.'"
          - "The framing introduces a 'Renaissance Developer' concept (drawn from Werner Vogels' 2026 CTO predictions): engineers who synthesise across domains, understand systems end-to-end, and exercise judgment about where AI helps and where it doesn't. Notably, engineers who can articulate where AI is *not* useful are described as demonstrating good judgment rather than failing to adopt. Leaders are expected to use AI hands-on in production work, not just manage it secondhand."
          - "Amazon uses 10th/90th percentile AI adoption rates as diagnostic signals — indicators of whether teams are under-exploring or over-indexing — not as performance targets. This is a meaningful distinction: it rejects AI adoption as a KPI while using adoption patterns as a health signal. The guidelines connect directly to [Anthropic's Expectations Gap (Oct 18 edition)](/newsletter/2026-10-18/) — where measured productivity outcomes consistently trailed employer/worker AI expectations — and [Anthropic's 'What Work Can Robots Do?' (Oct 4 edition)](/newsletter/2026-10-04/). Amazon's answer to those findings: lead with outcomes, not tools."
